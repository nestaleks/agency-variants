    05 / FAQ

    Questions buyers ask first

    Why are prices not shown?

    Prices and availability change as each building progresses. Send an enquiry and we’ll share the current price list for the apartments you’re interested in.

    Can I buy if I don’t live in Spain?

    Yes. We support you at every stage — from selecting the property to closing the deal — including legal support. If you rent the property out, the process can run without your direct involvement.

    Do you only sell your own buildings?

    No. We also help clients find land, buy or sell property, evaluate it, renovate it, or build a custom home turnkey.

    Since when has TID group been working in Mallorca?

    Since 2018. Two of our buildings — S’Olivar in Port de Sóller and Boho Corner in El Terreno — are completed and sold.
