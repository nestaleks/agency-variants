# Business & creative strategy

**Company:** Top Invest Development Group SL (TID group), Palma de Mallorca. Active since 2018.

**Offer:** boutique residential developments (7–9 units) in Palma and Port de Sóller, plus end-to-end services: land selection & purchase, design, construction, sales, rental, valuation, legal & process support, repair/renovation, investment consulting.

**Audiences**
- Primary: international and Spanish buyers of a home / second home in Mallorca (catalog tone: lifestyle, wellbeing, sea, privacy).
- Secondary: investors seeking capital preservation and rental income (site: Investments page; catalog closing line).
- Tertiary: landowners/sellers needing sales, valuation or construction services.

**Value proposition (evidence-based):** "Experiencia que construye confort" — a small developer that controls the whole chain (plot → design → build → sale/rent) and delivers boutique buildings in characterful Mallorcan locations, with quality control at every stage.

**Desired perception:** Mediterranean, calm, reliable, premium-but-approachable.

**Conversions**
- Primary: *Request availability & prices* for a project/unit (form → email).
- Secondary: call / email; investment consultation request; services enquiry (selling / building).

**Trust signals available:** since 2018 / 7+ years; 2 completed & sold buildings (S'Olivar, Boho Corner); 3 in progress; real photos and plans; named partners Blanc i Blanc and RBK Prime; company legal name and Palma address.

**Differentiators:** boutique scale; full-cycle capability; locations (sea-front El Arenal, Port de Sóller, El Terreno, central Palma); private pools/rooftops.

**Essential functionality to preserve:** project overviews, galleries, unit details with plans, contact (phone/email), partners links, EN default.

**IA used by concepts:** Hero → Positioning → Projects (status-aware) → Services/approach → Investment → Mallorca/locations → Partners → Contact form → Footer.
