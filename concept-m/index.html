    Section 5. Considerations for investors

    Real estate in Spain as a placement of capital — and what decides the value of a home.

    5.1 Factors of demand

    Investing in real estate is considered one of the prime options for capital placement, and is generally regarded as relatively stable and reliable in the long term.1 Many factors influence demand. The key ones:

    1. aLocation
    2. bA thoughtful design concept
    3. cQuality of infrastructure
    4. dAttractiveness of public spaces
    5. eEfficient interaction with the management company

    5.2 Ownership from abroad

    Commercial premises are offered within TID group buildings, with help in renting them out. If you live in another city or country, the process can proceed without your direct involvement.

    For investors, TID group offers investment consulting and financial services, legal support and support at every stage of the transaction (see 3.9–3.12).
